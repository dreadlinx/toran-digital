Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a logo cost?
Logo packages start from R2,500 for a basic logo with 3 concepts. Brand identity packages including guidelines and stationery are quoted separately.
How long does logo design take?
First concepts are delivered within 5–7 business days. Final delivery after approvals typically takes 2–3 weeks total.
Can I use my logo on merchandise?
Yes — you receive full commercial usage rights and vector files suitable for embroidery, screen printing, and signage.
What if I don't like any of the concepts?
We offer a free redesign round if none of the initial concepts align with your vision. We don't stop until you're happy.
