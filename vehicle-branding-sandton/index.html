Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a vehicle wrap last?

Our high-quality cast vinyl wraps typically last between 5 to 7 years in South African weather conditions.

Do you design the wrap?

Yes, our graphic design team will create a custom, eye-catching design before any printing begins.

Can I wrap a leased vehicle?

Absolutely. Vehicle wraps actually protect the original paintwork and can be safely removed before returning the lease.
