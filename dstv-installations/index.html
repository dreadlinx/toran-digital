What We Install & Fix

Every Home Tech Service Under One Roof

One call, one team, sorted. We handle everything from a new DSTV dish to a full CCTV system — no need to chase multiple contractors.

New DSTV Installations

Brand new dish and decoder setup — Explora, HD, and Extra View configurations. We align, test, and make sure every channel loads cleanly before we leave.

🔧

Signal Repairs & Troubleshooting

Stuck on "No Signal" or "E48-32" error? We diagnose the problem fast — whether it's a misaligned dish, damaged cable, or a faulty LNB — and fix it on the spot.

Professional TV Mounting

Wall-mounted TVs that look like they belong there. We handle the bracket installation, cable concealment, and make sure your TV sits level, secure, and at the right height.

CCTV Camera Installation

Protect what matters. We install high-definition indoor and outdoor camera systems for homes and businesses, with remote viewing setup on your phone so you're always in the know.

DSTV Relocations

Moving to a new house or just rearranging a room? We relocate your existing decoder and dish cleanly, with proper cable runs so it doesn't look like an afterthought.

Extra View & Multi-Room Setup

Want DSTV in more than one room? We set up Extra View and multi-room configurations properly so everyone watches what they want, when they want — no signal splitting.
